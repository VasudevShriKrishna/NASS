   433→# PHASE 7 — ADD CLOSED-LOOP DECISION MANAGEMENT
   434→
   435→The control tower must go beyond displaying routes and sending alerts.
   436→
   437→Implement a closed-loop workflow that turns an operational exception into an assigned, traceable, and verifiably closed action.
   438→
   439→Use this lifecycle:
   440→
   441→1. **Detect:** Identify a delay, unexpected port event, missing document, quantity discrepancy, stale update, customs hold, or other validated exception.
   442→2. **Verify:** Retrieve supporting events and documents. Check the timestamps, data source, and shipment reference.
   443→3. **Assess:** Explain the operational impact, affected material, potentially affected project, urgency, and uncertainty.
   444→4. **Recommend:** Suggest a clearly justified next step. Separate the recommendation from any decision already approved.
   445→5. **Assign:** Record the responsible team or person, agreed deadline, and priority.
   446→6. **Approve:** Obtain the required authorized approval for decisions or external actions.
   447→7. **Execute:** Record the action taken through the relevant connected system or verified manual workflow.
   448→8. **Verify outcome:** Check whether the original issue was actually resolved using a new event, document, quantity reconciliation, or responsible-person confirmation.
   449→9. **Close:** Store the closure evidence, timestamp, owner, and final status.
   450→
   451→Each action must have a unique ID, linked shipment or exception, owner, due date, status, supporting evidence, and closure history.
   452→
   453→Do not mark an alert resolved simply because a user dismissed it. Do not send messages, alter purchase orders, authorize payments, or commit external business actions without the required permission and approval.
   454→
   455→Show the outstanding exceptions, decisions awaiting approval, overdue actions, verified closures, and unresolved blockers in the Decision Management module.
   456→
   457→The assistant should answer questions such as:
   458→
   459→- What operational decisions require attention today?
   460→- Why is this shipment flagged?
